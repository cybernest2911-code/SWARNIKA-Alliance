TRANSITION ROADMAP

Fourteen weeks, six phases

Legal continuity is preserved throughout: the CIN and financial history stay with the entity. The ROC name change is the pacing item; everything else runs in parallel around it.

Professional sign-off required. The MOA amendment, name change, novation strategy, and anything touching obligations to the Innowiz partners must be executed by a practising Company Secretary and lawyer. This blueprint is a working plan, not legal advice.
WEEKS 1–4 · LEGAL COUNSEL + YOU

Untangle Innowiz cleanly

Inventory every contract, retainer, and success-fee agreement held in Innowiz's name. Review the partnership deed for noncompete, non-solicit, and IP clauses. Decide per client: novation vs. fresh engagement letter under the new entity. Nothing is announced until this is mapped.

WEEKS 3–8 · COMPANY SECRETARY

Restructure the entity

Amend the MOA object clause to add management consulting, advisory, international trade facilitation, and business development (special resolution + MGT-14). File the RUN application for the name change to Swarnika International Private Limited; obtain the fresh Certificate of Incorporation. Reconstitute board and shareholding deliberately — this is the moment to design governance you control.

WEEKS 6–10 · CA / CS

Update registrations

GST, PAN/TAN records, bank accounts, FSSAI (name update, licence continuity), and obtain an Importer-Exporter Code for the trade-corridor work. File trademark applications for SWARNIKA INTERNATIONAL and SWARNIKA ALLIANCE (India, Class 35/36; EUIPO to follow).

WEEKS 6–10 · BRAND + WEB

Build the brand & website

Commission final logo artwork against the identity specification. Secure swarnikainternational.com and mail migration. Build the group website (pages 7–9); prepare rebranded deck, letterhead, engagement-letter and invoice templates.

WEEKS 10–12 · YOU PERSONALLY

Run the communication sequence

Private client letters first, contract novation second, public announcement last — the full package and drafts are on page 5. Brief the 27-partner channel network and Nordic stakeholders with updated collateral.

WEEKS 12–14 · GROUP

Go live & align globally

Website launch with redirects from swarnikaagro.com and innowizconsulting.com (where controllable). Formalise the Stavanger presence under the group's own name — a Norwegian branch (NUF) or a subsidiary such as Swarnika International Nordic AS — so Nordic contracts and revenue flow correctly to the group. First invoices issue under Swarnika International Private Limited.

COMMUNICATION PACKAGE

Growth story, never an exit story

The narrative is consolidation and scale — bringing the advisory practice and the agro-industrial business under one international group. Sequence is everything: no client should learn of the change from LinkedIn.
